# Welcome

'Growing together in wisdom and love; discovering life in all its fullness.'

Welcome to the website of Malmesbury C of E Primary School. We are a school of around 400 pupils, in the historic Wiltshire town of Malmesbury. Our vision statement above summarises everything we aim for: a sense of community, deep learning, emotional flourishing, excitement about learning and setting our children on a pathway to a fulfilling life.

This site is full of useful information for anyone thinking of joining us and showcases some of the amazing things which happen here.
