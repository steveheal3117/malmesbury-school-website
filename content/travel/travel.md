# Travel to Malmesbury Primary School

Malmesbury Primary School is located at post code SN16 9JR. What 3 words: shook.banquets.revival. The vehicular entrance, which is also one of three pedestrian entrances, opens off Poole Road. This entrance leads to a car park which is for deliveries, staff, disabled people and pre-arranged visitors only. It has a traffic barrier. The other pedestrian entrances are off Poole Road and via the Sillars Walk footpath to the west of the school.

We actively encourage our pupils and staff to walk, scoot or cycle to school in order to:
- keep us fit and healthy
- help our pupils develop road safety skills which will keep them safe as they travel further afield and more independently
- help keep our local air clean
- reduce inconvenience from congestion to our neighbours
- reduce danger from traffic flows

Further information, including maps of the entrances, the park & stride car parks and the rear footpath: [Travel to School information](/documents/Travel-to-school-information.pdf).
