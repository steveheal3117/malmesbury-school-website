# Payments

<!-- ARBOR_GRID -->

## ESC Clubs
ESC Football and Dodgeball payments are made by completing the registration form and making direct payments to the Club organiser.

## Music/dance/gym lessons
Please enquire about music, gym and dance lessons at the Office. Payment for extra-curricular lessons is either made via Arbor, which will be set up for you after enrolling your child, or directly to the class provider.
