# School Tours

Deciding where to send your child to school is one of those decisions in life that you really want to get right. We are blessed with many good schools in the Malmesbury area, so we recommend that you visit a number of them. For some people a big school like ours is ideal, but others prefer the smaller schools in the surrounding villages. Finding the right school for your child is what matters, and you probably won't know until you've been inside them.

We are offering open mornings for parents/carers and children to view our wonderful school and meet some of the staff.

Each opening morning session will start at 10:00am and last approximately an hour. Please arrive at reception just before 10:00am to allow time to sign in and be given a Visitor badge.

## Dates
We run open mornings for prospective parents/carers throughout the autumn term. Please contact the school office for the current list of dates and to check availability.

## To book a place
Email the school office at admin@malmesbury-pri.wilts.sch.uk with:
- Your name
- A contact phone number for you (only used if the session has to be cancelled at short notice)
- Your child's name
- Your child's date of birth
- The date or dates you are available to attend

You will receive an email confirmation of your booking shortly after your request is received. If the session you have requested is fully booked, you'll be offered the next available session where there is space.

Applications for a Reception year school placement starting September 2027 are currently open, with a deadline of 15th January. Applications are made via Wiltshire County Council: [Apply for a school place](https://www.wiltshire.gov.uk/article/1657/Apply-for-a-school-place).

The last day to apply for the first round consideration is 15th January 2027.

We look forward to meeting you soon.
