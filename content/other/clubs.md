# Clubs

We offer a variety of clubs before, during and after school. Letters are usually sent out at the start of term advising the details of each club. Most of the clubs can be paid for via ParentMail, although ESC Football and Dodgeball are paid directly to ESC via the office.

Club tabs: Gym · Netball · Cricket · JAM Club · Essential Skills Coaching (ESC) · Cookery · Rugby · LEGO

## Gym
Our Gym Club is run before school on Tuesday and Friday mornings, from 8.15 to 8.55. The instructor is Janine Stone.

## Netball
Netball club runs in some terms, after school in the ball court. Sessions are notified via ParentMail.

## Cricket
Cricket club usually is held on the field in terms 5 & 6. Sessions are notified via ParentMail.

## JAM Club
JAM club stands for Jesus and Me. It is a free lunchtime club for any child in year 3/4 which takes place in the Media Suite after the children have eaten their lunch.

The session aims to be fun with a variety of bible stories often involving drama, singing, crafts and games. All children are very welcome.

Contact: Rachel White

## Essential Skills Coaching (ESC)
Please pick up a form from the school foyer and return completed to the Office if you would like your child to take part in the ESC Football or Dodgeball clubs.
