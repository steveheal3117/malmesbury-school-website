# Attendance and Absence

## School hours
School hours are 08:50-15:20, Monday to Friday. Children are in school for a total of 32.5 hours a week.

## Sickness
If your child will be absent from school through illness, please let us know by using the Arbor App by 9:00 a.m. Medical appointments can be notified in the same way.

## All other absences and holiday requests
DfE regulations regarding absence no longer permit Headteachers to authorise any leave or absence from school unless the circumstances are 'exceptional' relating to a 'one off' isolated situation. Any such request must be submitted in good time using the [Absence Request Form](/documents/Leave-of-Absence-Request-Form-MPS.pdf) available to download below. Hard copies are also available in the school foyer.
