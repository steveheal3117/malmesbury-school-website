# School Uniform

**SCHOOL UNIFORM:**

- A red sweatshirt or cardigan
- A white or navy shirt or polo shirt
- Navy or black trousers, shorts, skirts, or pinafores
- Black shoes (No trainers)
- Red gingham dresses and closed toe sandals are permitted in the summer.

## Shoes
<!-- SHOES_IMAGE -->
Please ensure that your children wear sensible shoes to school. Children are very active during the day both inside the school and in the playground. Their shoes need to be comfortable and safe. "Strappy" sandals are likely to cause trips and falls and boots are not allowed – in extreme weather children will be allowed to wear boots/wellingtons for their journey into school and then change into school shoes. In the winter months some parents may prefer their children to wear ankle-boot style shoes. These should have a low or no heel, similar to the styles pictured.

## Hair
<!-- HAIR_IMAGE -->
As shown in the adjacent picture, hair below the shoulder should be tied up so that it does not cause a hazard in P.E and sports and remains out of eyes in lessons. Hair should be natural in colour and not dyed. We do not permit tramlines and encourage blending between the longer and shorter hair.

## Jewellery and Hair accessories
<!-- JEWELLERY_IMAGE -->
For safety reasons children should not wear jewellery. The only exceptions are stud earrings and essential religious items. We do not want to see children wearing hoops or dangly earrings. Please do not send your child to school with fancy headbands and clips – they are not part of school uniform. Please save these for weekends, weddings and parties!

## Smart Watches
<!-- SMARTWATCH_IMAGE -->
Children are not permitted to wear smart watches in school (watches with functionality beyond basic time-telling/date/stopwatch/alarm). Some smart watches can take photos/videos, send/receive texts, or connect to the internet — impossible to individually police which functions are active, so please keep these devices at home. Traditional analogue watches are encouraged for older children learning to tell the time.

## Make Up
Make up including nail varnish is not allowed in school.

## PE Kit
- A draw string bag (not plastic), left at school during term time, clearly named with child's name and class
- A pair of black shorts and a red t-shirt
- A pair of clearly named trainers
- A black/blue plain tracksuit or jogging top and trousers for outside PE
- Gymnastics and dance take place in the hall in bare feet; trainers used for outdoor games lessons
- Children needing to wear jewellery for religious purposes should provide tape or micropore tape to cover the jewellery during the lesson
