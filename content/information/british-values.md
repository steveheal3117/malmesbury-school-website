# Promoting British Values at Malmesbury Church of England Primary School

The DfE have reinforced the need "to create and enforce a clear and rigorous expectation on all schools to promote the fundamental British values of democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty and mutual respect and tolerance of those with different faiths and beliefs." The Government set out its definition of British values in the 2011 Prevent Strategy. At the heart of our work are our school values, reinforced regularly through our cycle of collective worship throughout the year. The house system reinforces promotion of these values — pupils who positively demonstrate them are rewarded with house points by all staff.

## Our Values
- **Love** — Love for myself, Love for others, Love for God
- **Respect** — I respect myself, treat others as I would like to be treated and value what I have around me
- **Kindness** — I am friendly, helpful and polite
- **Courage** — I have faith, I believe in myself and I rise to a challenge
- **Independence** — I am resilient, resourceful and reflective
- **Creativity** — I use my imagination to explore and share ideas

## Democracy
Pupils complete an annual survey on school life and have numerous opportunities to express views on aspects of school life (sometimes set up by SLT, Governors, or emerging from pupil-organised activities). Each new school year, pupils decide their class rules and associated rights, with all children contributing. Y6 pupils have termly house meetings to nominate new House Captains; staff consider nominations and the office is awarded on merit.

## The Rule of Law
The importance of rules and laws (class, school, or country) is consistently reinforced. Pupils are taught school rules from an early age, plus Playground Rules and Safety Rules for various activities — the value and reasons behind rules/laws, how they govern and protect us, responsibilities in upholding them, and consequences when broken. Visits from authorities such as the Police and Fire Service reinforce this.

## Individual Liberty
Pupils are actively encouraged to make choices in a safe and supportive environment, with clear boundaries fostered through an empowering, creative curriculum (IPC). Pupils are encouraged to know, understand and exercise their rights and personal freedoms safely — e.g. through eSafety and Learn 4 Life lessons.

## Mutual Respect
Mutual respect is at the heart of our school values. Pupils learn that their behaviours affect their own rights and those of others; all members of the school community treat each other with respect.

## Tolerance of those of Different Faiths and Beliefs
Diversity is actively promoted through celebrating different faiths and cultures. RE and Learn 4 Life lessons alongside the school's Christian foundation reinforce tolerance and respect. Links with clergy at Malmesbury Abbey and Foundation Governors reinforce this. Members of different faiths/religions are encouraged to share knowledge to enhance class/school learning; pupils visit places of worship or learn from visiting speakers about different faiths.
