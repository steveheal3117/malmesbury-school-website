---
title: Dyslexia Friendly Schools
---

In July 2022, our school was one of only two in the whole of Wiltshire to achieve Dyslexia Friendly School status. Building on this, we successfully maintained our dyslexia friendly school status as a result of the reverification process which took place in December 2025. This is a huge achievement for our whole school community and has enabled us to raise dyslexia awareness, ensure continuity in terms of understanding and provision as well as to celebrate learning differences to promote positive self-worth in all of our young people.

Click [HERE](https://www.wiltshire.gov.uk/news/wiltshire-schools-successful-in-achieving-nationally-recognised-dyslexia-friendly-quality-mark) to read the press release.
