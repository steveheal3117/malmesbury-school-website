# PE and Sport Premium

The PE and Sports Premium is additional government funding to enable schools to make improvements in sports provision and support healthy lifestyles. Here at Malmesbury the funding has been used to raise the quality and breadth of PE and sport provision, to enable and encourage all children to participate in, and enjoy, sport.

Our school vision of 'Growing together in wisdom and love; discovering life in all its fullness' is exemplified in our PE provision as we ensure that pupils can participate in a wide range of sports and games, so that they can find what they enjoy and create a positive foundation for a healthy lifestyle.

This year, the grant is being removed by the government from Easter, and replaced with centralised organisation and funding. The statement below details how we used the grant in 2025-26. This year, we will continue to provide similar support until the grant ends.

**3. Targeted Student Demographics**

- **Disadvantaged Pupils & Pupil Premium:**  
  *Disadvantaged and pupil premium children were given preferential access to after school sports clubs. Some were also subsidised to support the cost of attending sports clubs. All received substantial support with the cost of outdoor and adventurous activities when these were delivered through residential visits. All costs of participation in inter-school events were met through pupil premium.*

- **Pupils with SEND or Medical Conditions:**  
  Children with a variety of SEND needs were fully accommodated in school sports lessons, clubs, teams, and sports day. This required the provision of additional equipment and staffing.

- **Girls' Participation Initiatives:**  
  A Girls only football club was run by the school, and a netball club was also reserved exclusively for girls.

    The school waived charges for the local girls’ football teams to use our grounds for training.

    Girls were selected to be Sports Leaders, championing sport across the school to inspire and assist younger children. These children received a sports leadership course delivered at an external venue by a specialist provider.

**4. Partnerships**

- **PE/School Sport Partnerships (e.g., local clusters, active partnerships):**  
  The school participated in a local sports cluster of 10 schools. A large number of inter-school competitions were organised by the sports coordinator, who was jointly appointed by the schools. Sport Premium covered staffing, travel and organisational costs.

**5. Year 6 Swimming & Water Safety Data**

- **Percentage who can swim competently over a distance of at least 25 metres:** 83 % (National Average 72%)

- **Percentage who can use a range of strokes effectively (e.g., front crawl, backstroke, breaststroke):** 68% (National Average 50%)

- **Percentage who can perform safe self-rescue in different water-based situations:** 76% (National average 71%)

> **Impact:** Our swimming programme ran through years 4 to 6 this year, with each class going for half a term. Three professional coaches were employed for each session while school staff were in a support capacity, which makes the programme highly effective. The children were grouped by ability and all made good progress. A number of our children have private swimming lessons and are already experienced when they begin school lessons. However, for our disadvantaged learners this is often their first encounter with swimming lessons and for some of them their first encounter with swimming at all. Additional sessions were also organised during the summer term for children in Year 6 who had not reached the national standard.
