# Academy Information

Malmesbury CE Primary School is a Single Academy Trust. This means that we are neither controlled by Wiltshire Local Authority nor are we part of a Multi-Academy Trust. We have the independence to make decisions which are right for our school and our community. We adhere to all the financial reporting regulations expected of a multi-academy trust.
