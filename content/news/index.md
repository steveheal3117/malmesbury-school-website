Many thanks to the FOMPS who paid for this special treat!

**January 2026:** Reverification of Dyslexia Friendly School status.

We are very proud to announce that our work to embed dyslexia friendly teaching has been recognised. We successfully maintained our dyslexia friendly school status after the recent reverification process which took place in December 2025.

The Dyslexia Friendly Quality Mark is issued to educators that can demonstrate that they provide high quality education and practice for dyslexic individuals, indicating effective practice in supporting pupils with dyslexia and literacy difficulties.
