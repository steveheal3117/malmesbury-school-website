Our SENDCo, Laura Phillips, will be running an interactive session on ADHD in the Autumn term, and there are some information resources for you to have a look at below:

- Let's Talk About ADHD
- What is ADHD | ADHD Awareness Month 2026
- What is ADHD – The Amazing Things Project
