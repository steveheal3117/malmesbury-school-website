title: Autism Acceptance Month 2026
---

April is Autism Acceptance month.

At least one in every 100 people in the UK are autistic. This could be your child, or your child's friend or classmate. As a parent or carer, there's a lot you can do to understand autism and help your child show up for their autistic classmates and friends.

There is a useful video you can watch with your child, describing how children feel, in their own words.

And a leaflet, with useful information too. Please take a look.
