Our school is a 'two form entry' school which means we have two classes in every year group. We structure our classes into 'Mileposts' so that teachers can support one another by planning in teams. The Reception classes are collectively known as EYFS (Early Years Foundation Stage). Milepost 1 comprises the Year 1 and Year 2 pupils. Milepost 2 consists of Year 3 and Year 4 and Milepost 3 contains Years 5 and 6.
