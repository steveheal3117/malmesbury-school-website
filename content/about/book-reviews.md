# Book Reviews

Just finished a book that has really inspired you? Why not write a review to let other people know how good it was! We will be posting reviews on this page, so if you're stuck for which book to choose next why not browse through the page to help you decide!

## Reviews found
**Candy Floss** by Jacqueline Wilson — "This book is AMAZING although it is very sad. It is about a girl called Flora who has a once in a lifetime opportunity to go to Sydney! But she chooses to stay with her dad who is very poor. He is so poor he nearly becomes homeless but Billy the Chip comes to the rescue! Flora loves the Fair especially Pearl the Merry go round Horse and her friend Rose the CANDY FLOSS lady. Will Rose save them? I would recommend this book to children over 6 because it is very sad and one part is quite scary." — **By Hannah**
