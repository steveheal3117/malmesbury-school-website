# OFSTED & SIAMS Reports

Church Schools are inspected twice: by Ofsted and by the Church.

## Ofsted

We had our most recent **Ofsted** inspection in May 2024.

A few highlights:

> "The needs of children are at the heart of this school, both academically and socially."

> "Behaviour is exemplary across the school. Pupils show high levels of engagement and interest in their learning."

> "Pupils rise up to meet the high expectations set by staff of their behaviour. They have a secure understanding of routines enabling the school to be calm and orderly."

> "Staff expertise ensures pupils with SEND progress successfully alongside their peers."

> "Parents praise the care provided to pupils, especially those with SEND."

> "Bullying does not happen; pupils feel safe and know they can talk to someone if they have a concern."

## SIAMS Report

The school had its "Statutory Inspection of Anglican and Methodist Schools" in the summer of 2026.

Highlights from this report include:

> "The vision drives an exceptional culture of compassionate care and encouragement in which barriers to learning are overcome."

> "the vision infuses a spark that ignites inspiration within its community members"

> "Due to the exemplary individual as well as class support, pupils thrive in their learning."

> "The school is a celebration of diversity and harmony, where people are respected as unique individuals and come together in dignity."
