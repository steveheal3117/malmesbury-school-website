# Governors

Governing bodies are responsible for working with the school to ensure that it delivers a good quality education. Together with the Headteacher, who is responsible for day-to-day management, the governors set the school's aims and policies.

**Key roles of governors:** ensure clarity of vision/ethos/strategic direction; ensure accountability for educational performance and staff performance management; oversee financial performance and value for money; ensure stakeholder voices are heard; support/challenge as "critical friend".

**Other duties:** determining budget spend; appointing/dismissing staff; hearing appeals and grievances; setting the policies on curriculum and collective worship; setting pupil behaviour/discipline standards; ensuring buildings are welcoming and safe; setting/monitoring aims and other policies.

For further information about the work of the governing body and its committees please see our [Scheme of Delegation 2026-27](/documents/MPS-Scheme-of-Delegation-2026-27.pdf).

You can contact our Chair of Governors, Laurence Mussett, by email at [chair@malmesbury-pri.wilts.sch.uk](mailto:chair@malmesbury-pri.wilts.sch.uk).

## How do I become a governor?

The numbers and types of governors are determined by our **[articles of association](/documents/Malmesbury-CofE-Articles-of-Association.pdf)**. As vacancies arise they are advertised, but you can also register your interest or find out more about the role by contacting the Clerk to the Governors. The address is: **[clerk@malmesbury-pri.wilts.sch.uk](mailto:clerk@malmesbury-pri.wilts.sch.uk)**

## Members (Academy Trust)

Malmesbury CE Primary is a Single Academy Trust. As charitable companies limited by guarantee, every academy trust has Members who have a similar role to the shareholders in a company limited by shares.
