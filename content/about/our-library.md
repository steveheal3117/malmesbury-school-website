# Our library

Here at Malmesbury Primary School we are lucky enough to have two library spaces, and an extensive selection of lovely books. In the Reception and Year 1 area we have the Storyland library, where the walls are decorated with much-loved book characters.

Upstairs in the Junior Library the books are organised by author surname for fiction, and by subject for the non-fiction shelves.
