Please work with your child on their reading at home; we would encourage you to listen to your child read several times a week.

By listening to your child read, you will be helping to improve their fluency. You can also encourage them to put expression into their reading and question them on the book to check their comprehension.

Follow the links to our letter with suggestions for comprehension questions, our suggested reading list, and the Reading Rockets website (additional reading suggestions under the Children's Books and Authors section).
