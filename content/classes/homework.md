# Homework

Homework is intended to provide opportunities for parents and carers to be involved in the learning process. It enables children to practice and consolidate skills and provides enrichment and extension opportunities.

It aims to enable children to take responsibility for their own learning, to become independent learners and to develop perseverance.

This page shows on which days your child will receive their homework. In addition to this homework, please provide a regular opportunity to either listen to your child read, or engage with them about a text they are currently reading.

Homework is usually sent out by Arbor, and/or accessed through the specific class pages.
