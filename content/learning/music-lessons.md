# Music Lessons

We offer a variety of music lessons, with teaching provided by external tutors. Usually parents make arrangements and payment for lessons through direct contact with our tutors.

If you would like your child to begin music lessons please email the Office: admin@malmesbury-pri.wilts.sch.uk and we will put you in touch with a tutor.
